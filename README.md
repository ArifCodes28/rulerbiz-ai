# RuralBiz AI

### AI-Powered Hyper-Local Business Advisory & Growth Assistant

RuralBiz AI is a smart business advisory platform designed to help rural entrepreneurs make better business and financial decisions using hyper-local insights, deterministic financial calculations, and AI-powered recommendations.

## 🎯 Problem

Rural entrepreneurs often face difficulty in:

- Identifying suitable businesses for their local area
- Understanding local demand and competition
- Finding market gaps and opportunities
- Choosing suitable financing options
- Understanding loan repayment requirements
- Identifying opportunities to expand and grow their business

## 💡 Solution

RuralBiz AI combines hyper-local business analysis with financial planning and an AI-powered growth advisor.

The platform helps entrepreneurs:

1. Analyze business feasibility for their location
2. Understand demand, competition, risks and market gaps
3. Calculate project cost and financing requirements
4. Identify suitable financing/scheme options
5. Receive personalized business growth recommendations
6. Discover relevant business opportunities
7. Receive daily business intelligence
8. Identify customers who may require follow-up

## 🚀 Key Features

### Hyper-Local Business Feasibility
- Demand analysis
- Customer reach
- Competition analysis
- Market gap identification
- SWOT analysis
- Risk assessment
- Business opportunity scoring

### Smart Financial Engine
- Project cost estimation
- Loan requirement calculation
- Financing/scheme routing
- Interest and tenure calculation
- Repayment planning

> Financial calculations are rule-based and deterministic to improve transparency and reliability.

### AI Business Advisor
- Personalized business recommendations
- Growth strategies
- Expansion suggestions
- Simple explanations of complex business and financial information

### Opportunity Discovery
- Tender/bid opportunities
- New market opportunities
- Online selling opportunities
- Relevant government/business schemes

### Customer Follow-up Suggestions
The system identifies customers who may be worth contacting based on business patterns and provides suggested follow-up actions.

> The prototype does not automatically call customers.

### Daily Business Intelligence
- Relevant business news
- Market updates
- New opportunities
- Actionable recommendations
